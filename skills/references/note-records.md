# Note records

Read this reference before creating, updating, or removing an agent note in `notes/`.
Ask: “What future decision would this change, and where else is that information preserved?”
Record a note only if it prevents a concrete mistake or repeated investigation and has no maintained owner already preserving it.

## Choose what to record

Useful notes contain unresolved findings with affected files, evidence, and remaining uncertainty;
decision-changing validation limits; or otherwise-lost rationale and provenance.
Exclude session recaps, completed-work lists, test-count receipts, generic process lessons,
temporary environment failures without continuing consequences, and speculative improvements without a concrete consumer or observed problem.
Do not duplicate maintained docs, tests, issue records, or existing notes.

## Preserve evidence

Record the finding, not a closeout narrative containing it.
Preserve provenance and distinguish observed facts from inference.
Make notes usable after temporary files disappear: include the decisive observation, known source revision or input identity,
and enough reproduction detail to investigate again.
Temporary logs may supplement a note, but must not be its only evidence.
State missing evidence rather than inventing it.

## Update or remove resolved notes

When later work resolves a recorded finding, update or remove it rather than adding another note announcing resolution.
Retain rationale or provenance only while it remains useful.
Do not create notes merely to close a turn.
Do not commit your notes; keep them as untracked files.
