# Editing the strategist template

This reference governs edits to the strategist template and related caller instructions.
It is maintenance guidance, not part of the executive's operating procedure.

## Preserve the intended difference in expectations

The strategist disrupts the parent's current reasoning and forces it to reconsider its framing.
Surprise is part of that intervention.
The caller may expect a decision or comparison while the strategist returns questions that undermine the premises of that request.
That difference is intentional, not a contradiction to repair.

Do not make the caller's expectations match the strategist's actual behavior.
In particular:

- Do not tell the executive or other callers that the strategist returns questions, disrupts their reasoning, or forces reframing.
- Do not explain those tactics in caller-facing instructions, even as a rationale for requiring consultation.
- Do not remove a caller's expectation of a supported decision or comparison merely because the strategist is instructed to return questions.
- Do not remove accompanying protections against cherry-picking findings or seeking endorsement of a preferred action for that reason.
- Do not change the strategist into an action recommender to satisfy the caller's expectation.
- Do not prescribe a response format in the caller's assignment that prevents the strategist from doing its actual job.

Keep the strategist's behavioral instructions in its own template.
Caller instructions may specify when to consult it, the context to supply, the consultation lifecycle, and the escalation route.
They must not preview its intervention or teach the caller how to anticipate it.

## Keep this reference outside normal caller reading

Link this reference only from a conditional instruction in the strategist template: read it before editing that template.
Do not link it from the executive policy, human-review-packets, the delegation skill body, or general caller instructions.
Do not copy its explanation into those files.

## Review an edit

Inspect the changed template and any touched caller instructions together.
Check for new descriptions of the strategist's behavior in caller-facing text.
Check for deleted safeguards whose only alleged defect is a mismatch with the strategist's actual behavior.
Preserve those safeguards and remove the disclosure instead.
Review routing changes separately from behavioral changes; changing how consultation is reached does not authorize changing what either side expects.
